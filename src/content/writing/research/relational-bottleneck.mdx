## Abstract

A central challenge for cognitive science is to explain how abstract concepts are acquired from limited experience. Here, we highlight a recently emerging line of work that suggests a novel reconciliation of empiricist and nativist approaches, by exploiting an inductive bias that we term the relational bottleneck. We review a family of models that employ this approach to induce abstractions in a data-efficient manner, emphasizing their potential as candidate models for the acquisition of abstract concepts in the human mind and brain.
