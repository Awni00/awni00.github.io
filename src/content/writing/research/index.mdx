This hub collects paper pages and research notes around a common question: what structure, supervision, and statistical principles allow learning systems to generalize beyond their training distribution?

The current threads are complementary. Some papers study architectural mechanisms for relational or algorithmic reasoning in neural networks; others develop statistical frameworks for understanding when additional supervision or information structure changes sample complexity.

## Main Threads

- [Recursive latent-space reasoning](/writing/research/algorithmic-generalization-transformer-architectures): mechanisms for out-of-distribution algorithmic generalization in Transformers.
- [Chain-of-thought information](/writing/research/cot-info): a learning-theoretic measure of the statistical value of CoT supervision.
- [Dual Attention Transformers](/writing/research/dual-attention): an architectural separation between sensory and relational information in Transformer attention.
- [Relational convolutions](/writing/research/relational-convolutions): a neural operation for learning hierarchical relational representations.
- [Abstractors](/writing/research/abstractor): relational cross-attention as an inductive bias for explicit relational reasoning.

The graph view above is the canonical organization of these pages. Old standalone project URLs redirect into this hub so the writing corpus remains the source of record.
