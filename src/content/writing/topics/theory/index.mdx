progress in machine learning has at times looked more like alchemy than science.
progress in machine learning has been driven largely by empirical and engineering efforts; understanding has often lagged behind progress, and the gap has widened over time.
i believe in a scientific approach towards machine learning progress.
in my view, formal mathematical theory is one way 
i equally believe in developing understanding through empirical science (like physics and other natural sciences; viewing AI as a natural phenomenon to be studied, deriving *empirical laws*, etc.)

my philosophy about role of theory in ai research
