I'm a PhD candidate in the [Department of Statistics & Data Science at Yale University](https://statistics.yale.edu/), advised by [John Lafferty](https://www.lafferty-lab.org/).

I work on the foundations of artificial intelligence, aiming to identify simple, unifying principles that explain how intelligent systems learn.
I'm especially interested in what enables a model to learn complex tasks <Term note="With as little data, compute, and external supervision as possible.">efficiently</Term> and to <Term note="Extending to out-of-distribution problems: longer, harder, or composed in new ways.">generalize beyond its experience</Term>.
I approach these questions as both an <Term note="Experiments designed to isolate and explain a phenomenon, not just improve a benchmark.">empirical science</Term> and a <Term note="Formal theory that explains behavior from statistical and computational principles.">mathematical science</Term>, developing new architectures, algorithms, and theoretical frameworks. 

My work is organized across three interconnected threads:
- **[Architectural inductive biases](/blog/topics/inductive-biases):** what mechanisms an architecture needs for models to learn efficiently and form robust abstractions<HoverNote cite="altabaa2024disentangling" /><HoverNote cite="webbRelationalBottleneckInductive2023" /><HoverNote cite="altabaa2025unlockingoutofdistributiongeneralizationtransformers" />.
- **[Reasoning in Transformers & LLMs](/blog/topics/reasoning):** how models learn, represent, and execute multi-step reasoning, and how to generalize beyond training<HoverNote cite="altabaa2025unlockingoutofdistributiongeneralizationtransformers" /><HoverNote cite="altabaa2026uncoveringlatentreasoningstrategies" /><HoverNote cite="altabaa2025cotinformation" />.
- **[Theoretical foundations](/blog/topics/theory):** precise accounts of how architecture, learning algorithm, and problem structure determine what models can learn<HoverNote cite="altabaa2026transformertheories" /><HoverNote cite="altabaaRoleInformationStructure2024" /><HoverNote cite="altabaaApproximationRelationFunctions2024" />.

To learn more, explore my **[blog](/blog)**, which maps out my work across these interconnected threads.
